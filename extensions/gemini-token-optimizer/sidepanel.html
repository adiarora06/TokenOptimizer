Before you capture, type, or prepare

After you agree, prompt text you type or explicitly capture is handled in this side panel. Clicking Prepare securely sends the prompt to Token Optimizer's Vercel-hosted service for deterministic processing; no AI model receives it during preparation, and raw or prepared prompt text is not stored. Token counts and strategy are shown only in the current side-panel session. The service and its hosts process basic request data such as IP address, time, route, and status for delivery, security, and rate limiting; a pseudonymous rate-limit identifier may be retained briefly. The identity of the supported assistant stays in your browser.
